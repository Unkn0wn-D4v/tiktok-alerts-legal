TikTok Alerts

A Discord bot that watches TikTok accounts around the clock and alerts your server the moment one goes live or posts a new video — with a neon alert card, an optional @everyone / @here / role mention, and a summary once the stream ends.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok or Discord.
